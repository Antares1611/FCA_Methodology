Why 200 : 50 : 5 Tier Weights Matter in MH3SFCA / Huff Models: In standard 3SFCA (Wan et al., 2012), a Commune Health Station (Sj = 5) and a Provincial General Hospital (Sj = 200) located at the same travel time receive the exact same selection probability Gij. Impedance Sensitivity: Absolute Access (SPAI) vs. Spatial Access Ratio (SPAR)

Wan et al. (2012) proved that raw Spatial Access Index (SPAI) scores fluctuate wildly when β changes, whereas normalizing by the regional mean (SPAR = Ai / Mean(A)) stabilizes spatial patterns.

How β is Calibrated Mathematically: Following Wan et al. (2012) and Subal et al. (2021), β is solved by setting the Gaussian weight Wij = exp(-d02 / β) = 0.01 at the catchment boundary d0. Rearranging gives β = -d02 / ln(0.01) = d02 / 4.605. For d0 = 60 min, β = 3600 / 4.605 ≈ 781.7. For d0 = 30 min, β = 900 / 4.605 ≈ 195.4.

2SFCA
Luo & Wang (2003)		 Binary step (1 inside d0, 0 outside) None (unweighted population sum) Yes Baseline policy standards (e.g., official physician-to-population thresholds) where internal travel time is negligible.
E2SFCA / KD2SFCA
Luo & Qi (2009); Dai (2010)		 Stepwise or continuous Gaussian Wij Distance-weighted population only Yes General regional studies with moderate facility density; suffers from demand inflation in cities and weight cancellation in 1-clinic rural zones.
3SFCA
Wan et al. (2012)		 Stepwise or continuous Gaussian Wij Travel-time selection probability Gij Yes Urban & mixed regions with overlapping catchments: Prevents counting the same population multiple times across clustered clinics.
M2SFCA
Delamater (2013)		 Squared weight Wij2 in numerator Distance-weighted population No (penalizes suboptimal distance) Emergency services, rural isolation & facility siting: Penalizes long travel times even when a clinic has zero competitors.
MH3SFCA / Huff
Luo (2014); Subal (2021)		 Continuous Gaussian Wij Huff probability (Capacity Sj × Wij) Yes Tiered healthcare hierarchies (e.g., 200:50:5): Models patients bypassing small commune clinics for major provincial hospitals.
V2SFCA / MM2SFCA
Luo & Whippo (2012); Mao (2013)		 Continuous mode/catchment specific Subgroup or variable buffer split Yes Transport equity & urban-rural extremes: Compares ambulance vs. motorbike/boat speeds or dynamic rural travel thresholds.
